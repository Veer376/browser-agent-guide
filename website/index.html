WHAT'S ACTUALLY SHOWN IN THE RECORDING? ＋

Playwright inspects the heading, navigates to the booking form, selects a visit and time, types fictional patient information, verifies the selected date, changes Monday 12 October to Tuesday 13 October, reviews the updated state and confirms a practice appointment.
