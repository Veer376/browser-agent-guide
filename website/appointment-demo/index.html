GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Is Morrow a real clinic? +

No. Morrow is a fictional clinic created as a safe, realistic test environment for browser automation. You can try the entire booking flow without transmitting personal information.

Will my appointment actually be booked? +

No. The form runs entirely in this browser. Submitting it only shows a local confirmation screen.

Can I change my date before I confirm? +

Absolutely. Choose a day and time, enter your details, then use “Change details” from the review screen. Your entries remain available while you make changes.

What is this experience for? +

This practice booking page is a realistic target for the Browser Agent Guide Playwright skill: scrolling, clicking, inspecting, typing, verifying dates and testing outcomes.
