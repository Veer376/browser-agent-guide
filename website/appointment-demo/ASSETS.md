# Morrow demo assets

Morrow is a fictional care brand and demo experience. No appointment information is transmitted.

Three photographs were obtained from Unsplash and stored locally in `assets/` to avoid runtime dependencies:

- `clinic.jpg` — [Unsplash image 1497366216548-37526070297c](https://unsplash.com/photos/1497366216548-37526070297c), used as an illustrative contemporary interior, not a photograph of a healthcare facility
- `doctor.jpg` — [Unsplash image 1559839734-2b71ea197ec2](https://unsplash.com/photos/1559839734-2b71ea197ec2), an illustrative portrait and **not the named fictional clinician**
- `interior.jpg` — [Unsplash image 1519494026892-80bbd2d6fd0d](https://unsplash.com/photos/1519494026892-80bbd2d6fd0d), illustrative healthcare/support imagery

Images are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). All photographs are illustrative; page copy, clinic and clinician names are fictitious. Do not repurpose the images as proof of a real practice, staff affiliation or actual patient treatment.
