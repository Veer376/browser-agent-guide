# Community conduct

Keep discussions constructive and focused on the software. Contributors and maintainers should treat other participants respectfully, avoid harassment and discriminatory conduct, and respect confidentiality when reviewing vulnerabilities and bug reports.

Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, threats, spam and publication of private information are not. Maintainers may moderate or remove abusive participation, and contributors can raise concerns privately with maintainers through project contact channels when those are published.
